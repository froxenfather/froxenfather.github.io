About

Bridging the gap between code, humanity, and AI.

What I build

Things that touch the physical world or that people actually use: autonomous navigation software for utility-inspection drones at Los Alamos National Laboratory, an Android app for a roofing contractor, a first-person physics controller in Unity built from scratch instead of using Rigidbody.

What I care about

Machine learning and autonomous systems, specifically the parts where a model or a robot has to keep working outside a clean simulation. I care less about a benchmark score than about whether the thing still works when reality doesn't match the sim.

How I work

Scope the work before touching it, delegate what can run in parallel, and write down decisions and failures as I go instead of relying on memory. That habit came out of drone research where an undocumented failure just got repeated.

Right now

Working through an M.S. in Computer Science (AI/ML) at Colorado School of Mines and finishing a software engineering internship at Checkr. Outside of that, slowly building a freelance game called Hyperstimulant.

Outside code

I'm the Recording Secretary for my fraternity chapter and founded its Graduate Relations committee. I also spent a semester at the University of Canterbury in New Zealand, which reshaped how I think about problems outside a pure CS lens.

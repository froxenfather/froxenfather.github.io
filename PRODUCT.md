## Stack

Static HTML and CSS only, deployed to GitHub Pages (`froxenfather.github.io`). No JavaScript, framework, build step or external fonts unless the owner asks (see `CLAUDE.md`, `GOAL.md`).

## Users

Ryan Freese's personal site serves a wide mix: employers and recruiters, collaborators and peers, the course grader and classmates (P1, due Tue Sep 22, 2026), friends, family and general visitors. Owner's words: "Anyone, Employers, Family. Wide variety."

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that shows who Ryan is, what he can build, and how he thinks. Success: a visitor quickly understands his personality and technical ability, and can reach his best projects and his accounts.

## Product Principles

1. Evidence over claims: show concrete work, never adjectives.
2. One site, many audiences: a recruiter and a relative should both find their way in a few seconds.
3. Personality is a requirement, not decoration.
4. Placeholders stay visibly marked until the owner supplies real content.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Semantic HTML, sequential headings, visible keyboard focus, sufficient contrast, works from 375px up, honors reduced motion.
